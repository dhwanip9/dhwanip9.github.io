About me

Science, made operational

Creating meaningful impact is my north star. This surfaced during my PhD when I noticed a gap between the work I was doing in the lab and the potential impact it could have in the field. Every career turn since than has been in service of that goal.

Having lived and worked across India, Australia, Canada, and the United States, I bring a global perspective to high-stakes problem-solving. My career path spans the bench, scaling testing response during the pandemic, and venture execution - managing due diligence, business development, and portfolio strategy to help deep-tech startups scale efficiently.

I specialize in stepping into novel domains, synthesizing cross-functional complexities, and aligning cross-disciplinary teams toward a unified goal. I care deeply about operational clarity, resilient systems, and strategic work that empowers everyone around me to move faster and build smarter.
